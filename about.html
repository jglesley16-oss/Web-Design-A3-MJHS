About Our School

Mandi Junior High School is a junior high school serving students and the wider community in Turubu LLG of Wewak District in East Sepik Province, Papua New Guinea. The school aims to provide a positive environment where students can learn, develop their abilities and prepare for further education and responsible participation in society.

Education at the school focuses on academic learning, personal development, discipline, cooperation, cultural awareness and community responsibility.

Our Vision

To develop responsible, knowledgeable and confident young people who can contribute positively to their families, communities and the Country.

Our Mission

Mandi Junior High School is committed to creating a supportive learning environment that encourages students to achieve academic success while developing positive values, practical skills and responsible behaviour.
